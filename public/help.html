Help

Answers to common questions about using NotHub.

How do I read a note?

Open the Notes tab, pick a subject if you want to narrow the list, and click a note. When you finish, press "Mark as read" so it counts towards your progress on Home.

What does the day streak mean?

It is the number of days in a row that you opened a note or marked one as read. If you skip a whole day, the streak starts again from zero.

Where is my progress saved?

In your browser, on the device you are using. It stays when you close the page, but it does not move to another browser or another device.

Why did my progress disappear?

Progress is lost if you clear your browsing data or site data, use a private window, or open NotHub in a different browser or on a different device. Try the browser you used before.

What are DPP and Puzzles?

DPP stands for daily practice problems, to test what you have learned. Puzzles are short brain games. Both are coming soon.

I found a mistake in a note.

Thank you for spotting it. Send us an email with the note's title and what looks wrong, and we will fix it.

Still stuck?

Write to us and we will get back to you.
