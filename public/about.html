About NotHub

NotHub is a simple place for students to read notes, practise problems and train their brain with puzzles.

N

Notes

Clear notes organised by subject, with headings, lists and code examples.

Track your progress

The Home page shows how many notes you have read, how far you are in each subject, and your day streak, so it is easy to pick up where you left off.

NotHub is built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no frameworks. Made by Your Name.
